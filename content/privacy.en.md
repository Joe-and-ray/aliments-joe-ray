Les Aliments Joe & Ray Ltée ("Joe & Ray", "we") is committed to protecting personal information in accordance with Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, as amended by Law 25, and applicable Canadian privacy laws.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain and protect personal information when you use our website at alimentsjoeandray.com.

## Personal information we collect

When you send us a message through the contact form, we collect:

- Your name
- Your business name (optional)
- Your email address
- Your phone number (optional)
- Your message, and the language of the page you used

We also receive any personal information you choose to send us by phone or email.

Only if you accept analytics cookies, we collect anonymous statistics about your visit through Google Analytics: pages visited, time on the site, type of device and browser, approximate location (city or region) and how you arrived on the site. Google Analytics does not record or store your full IP address.

## Purposes of collection

We use personal information to:

- Answer your questions and requests, including pricing and account requests
- Set up and manage your account as a customer and process your orders
- Understand how the website is used and improve it (statistics, only with your consent)
- Meet our legal and regulatory obligations

We only use your information for the purposes described here, unless you consent to another use or the law permits it.

## Consent

We collect personal information with your consent, except where the law permits or requires otherwise. By sending us a message through the website, you consent to the use of your information for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Analytics cookies are off by default. They are only activated if you click "Accept" in the cookie notice.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us (see below), subject to legal or contractual limitations. To withdraw your consent to analytics cookies, click "Cookie preferences" at the bottom of any page and choose "Decline".

## Cookies and similar technologies

Our website uses:

- **Analytics cookies (only with your consent):** Google Analytics sets the cookies `_ga` and `_ga_JQ5V6RXPCJ`, which last up to 2 years, to count visits and understand how the site is used.
- **Your cookie choice:** we remember whether you accepted or declined in your browser's local storage, so the notice doesn't appear on every page. This is not shared with anyone.

You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie preferences" link at the bottom of every page. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

## Disclosure of personal information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We only share it with:

- **Service providers** who need it to provide services on our behalf: Web3Forms (delivers contact form messages to our inbox), Google (Google Analytics, only with your consent) and Cloudflare (website hosting and security)
- **Government authorities or other entities** when the law requires it

Our service providers are bound to protect personal information and to use it only for the services they provide to us.

## Information processed outside Quebec

Some of our service providers store or process information outside Quebec, including in the United States. Before using them, we assess whether the information will receive adequate protection, as required by Quebec law. Information processed outside Quebec may be subject to the laws of that jurisdiction.

## Retention and destruction

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes for which it was collected, unless the law requires a longer period. Contact form messages are kept for as long as needed to follow up on your request and, if you become a customer, for the duration of our business relationship. Google Analytics statistics are kept for no more than 14 months. Information is then securely destroyed or anonymized.

## Security measures

We use physical, administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal information against loss, theft and unauthorized access, disclosure or use. Our website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Despite these measures, no method of transmitting or storing data is completely secure.

## Confidentiality incidents

If a confidentiality incident involving personal information occurs, we will take reasonable steps to reduce the risk of harm, notify the Commission d'accès à l'information and the affected people when the incident presents a risk of serious harm, and record it in our register of confidentiality incidents, as required by Quebec law.

## Your rights

Under Quebec law, you have the right to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you
- Request the correction of inaccurate, incomplete or outdated information
- Withdraw your consent to the use of your personal information
- Receive the personal information you provided to us in a structured, commonly used technological format (data portability)
- Ask us to stop disseminating your personal information when the law allows it

To make a request, contact the person responsible below. We will respond in writing within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca).
